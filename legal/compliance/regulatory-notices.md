# Regulatory and Jurisdictional Notices

**Document status: jurisdiction-specific legal review required before publication.**

NIDAW operates a multi-product platform concept covering ride-hailing, accommodation, food delivery, freight/logistics and corporate travel. Legal and regulatory obligations depend on the jurisdictions, services and business entities actually launched.

Before enabling a product in a jurisdiction, obtain local review of applicable requirements, including:

- transportation operator, driver, vehicle, insurance and fare requirements for Nidus;
- accommodation licensing, consumer protection, tax and occupancy requirements for Haven;
- food business, merchant licensing, consumer protection and food-safety requirements for Vorax;
- freight/carrier licensing, customs, cargo insurance and transport documentation requirements for Logix;
- corporate travel, employment, procurement and tax requirements for Corpus;
- privacy, data protection, payment services, AML/KYC, consumer refunds and electronic communications requirements applicable to the actual operating model.

Do not claim compliance with a named regulator, law or standard until the applicable entity, service scope, jurisdiction and evidence have been reviewed and approved. Autonomous-vehicle and drone-delivery operations are not represented as active products by this notice.
